# Visual Assets and Photo Engine

`owner.png` is the owner's supplied photograph, copied without alteration.

`template-*.png` are screenshots of the sixteen real CV designs generated by `capture-templates.cjs`.

Signature uses the locally bundled [Allura font](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/allura), with its SIL Open Font License in `vendor/Allura-OFL.txt`.

`sample-portrait.png` is a fictional gallery-only portrait created with the built-in imagegen tool. It is never added to visitor CVs. Final prompt:

> Create a photorealistic professional headshot of a fictional adult woman, about 28, shoulder-length dark brown wavy hair tucked behind one ear, medium warm skin tone, confident approachable subtle smile, dark charcoal crewneck top, shoulders and full head comfortably in frame with space above hair. Direct eye contact with camera, centered balanced composition, pale cool gray seamless studio background. Modern editorial portrait photography, realistic skin detail, natural soft window light, crisp, no heavy retouching, no props, no jewelry, no text, no logos. Square image intended as a sample portrait in a professional CV template gallery. This is a fictional person and not based on any supplied image.

`folio-hero.png` was generated using the built-in imagegen tool for the homepage. Final prompt:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: wide website homepage hero background for Folio, a modern CV builder. Create a polished photorealistic CGI product photograph, wide landscape 1536x1024 composition. A luminous sculptural arrangement of three thin frosted glass and white paper resume sheets standing at slight angles on a seamless charcoal-black studio surface. Actual resume visual structure: small portrait block, typographic lines, sections, teal rules, neat white document paper. Main document surfaces bright enough to inspect. Fine mint green and warm coral edge lighting suggests advanced AI technology and precision. Arrange documents primarily in the right half, while left half is pure quiet charcoal negative space for real website title overlay. Fill the full image naturally with no rectangular container or border. Restrained premium editorial aesthetic, tactile paper and brushed aluminum details, beautiful physically realistic shadows. No orbs, no floating balls, no bokeh, no robot, no brain, no purple or blue gradient, no logos, no legible words or text. This is a background photograph of the CV product, not a screenshot of a web page.

The photo editor uses [Cropper.js 1.6.2](https://github.com/fengyuanchen/cropperjs/tree/v1.6.2). Its JavaScript, CSS, and MIT license are included in `vendor/`, so image editing does not depend on a CDN being available to visitors.
